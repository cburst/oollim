# koreanocean scoring coefficients

Trained on: speechocean, korean. No private evaluation recordings or ratings were used in fitting. Original source labels were mapped to a 0–10 scale; Korean fluency/prosody share a source label. These are scoring coefficients, not corpus recordings.
