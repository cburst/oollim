Seven of 510 adjusted decisions differ between seeds and are marked with a dagger; these borderline comparisons do not establish a direction. The table reports seed-42 intervals; full-precision results for both seeds are released. Approximately 59 draws fall in each adjusted tail, so endpoints remain Monte Carlo approximations. Correction covers these contrasts, not the preceding model search or all historical experiments; inference conditions on the observed panel and fitted models. No private scores were used for fitting or calibration.
